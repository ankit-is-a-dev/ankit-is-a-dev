Music, comedy and personal

I make music, and I make people laugh.

I am Ankit Singh, a singer and content creator from Delhi. I started by posting song covers on my phone, and over time it grew into a small community of people who show up for the covers and stay for the comedy.

Today I run three accounts: one for comedy and daily content, one for song covers, and one for personal and lyrics reels. Each has a different audience, and all of it is public on Instagram.
